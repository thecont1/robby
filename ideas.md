# Robby visual direction

## Three approaches

### 1. Contact-sheet archaeology
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial image-lab interface that treats each compiled composition like an archival object: front, reverse, stamps, plates, and visible construction marks. The mood is precise, tactile, and reflective rather than conventionally “technical.”

**Probability:** 0.037

### 2. Signal-room instrument panel
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained field-monitor aesthetic using charcoal panels, calibrated grids, and fluorescent status marks to evoke a computational imaging workstation. It is a more operational and kinetic interpretation of explainable compilation.

**Probability:** 0.084

### 3. Museum label / generative atlas
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet white-cube composition with generous paper margins, specimen labels, and controlled color studies. This frames the compiler output as a small exhibition of process-led photographic artworks.

**Probability:** 0.021

## Chosen approach: Contact-sheet archaeology

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design informed by photographic contact sheets, conservation labels, and early desktop-publishing ephemera. The UI borrows from the physical front/back logic of a print, rather than a generic software dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. The obverse and reverse are co-equal image-objects: neither is a secondary “debug” thumbnail.
2. Every computational fact is rendered as a legible material trace: a label, coordinate, swatch, layer card, or provenance contour.
3. Asymmetry guides attention from the source script to the paired images, then into the process record.
4. Warm paper and ink-like surfaces soften the compiler’s machinery without obscuring its precision.

### Color Philosophy
The primary field is warm archival paper, so photographic colours remain vivid and honest. Charcoal acts as the mechanical ink, while a saturated vermilion carries authorship and active state. Electric cobalt and acid-lime appear only as data marks lifted from the supplied street photograph’s lights—evidence, not decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
An editorial “fold-out” board: a narrow metadata rail anchors the left edge, a large paired-image spread dominates the middle, and an annotated process ribbon unspools below. Sections use staggered offsets and rules rather than centered cards or a conventional dashboard grid.

### Signature Elements
1. A bold red reverse-side registration disc, used for selection and provenance markers.
2. Thin black crop-rule lines and small baseline metadata captions around images.
3. Source-colour chips that repeat exactly across the provenance map, process record, and active-layer state.

### Interaction Philosophy
Selection behaves like lifting a translucent acetate overlay from a contact sheet: clicking a layer makes its corresponding region and graph node unmistakably active. Hover feedback is crisp and physical, with quiet underlines and a small positional shift rather than ornamental glow.

### Animation
Panels enter with a short 180ms upward-and-fade motion using a strong ease-out, staggered by operation order. Active provenance highlights animate only opacity and outline thickness; they never pulse continuously. The entire system respects `prefers-reduced-motion` by rendering the final state immediately.

### Typography System
`DM Mono` carries code, coordinates, hashes, and technical labels. `Source Serif 4` provides the editorial title voice and framing captions. A compact sans-serif system stack is reserved for interface controls. Display headlines use tight tracking; technical values use tabular numerals.

### Brand Essence
**Robby is an explainable visual-composition compiler for image-makers who want the final picture and the evidence of how it came to be.**

Personality: forensic, tactile, generous.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and editorial; CTAs name the actual transformation; microcopy favours concrete evidence over abstraction.

> “Every picture has a reverse.”

> “Trace the light back to its source.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark uses a condensed, ink-stamp-like `ROBBY` with the two B counters treated as paired apertures. The symbol is a split registration disc—an offset red circle cut by a narrow vertical seam—suggesting obverse/reverse without text.

### Signature Brand Color
**Registration Vermilion** — `#E3442F`.
